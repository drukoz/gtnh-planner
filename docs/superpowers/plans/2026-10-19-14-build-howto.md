**Goal:** The user's report — *"what about the recipes for the electronic circuit where we are missing the molten tin recipe"* — fixed for the whole build list: every machine-made part of the "to build" list says what makes it, the build steps tell the group to make each one in order ("Make 4,032 L Molten Tin from 28× Tin Dust in a Fluid Extractor"), "we already make this" items stop being a cheap source the running line recycles into steel, structure cells that hold air or a fluid stop showing up as things to craft, and the build totals stop multiplying whole runs per part.

**Architecture:** Server: the build-list breakdown (`bom.ts` `expandWith`) chooses one recipe per good in a depth-first walk of its own — no depth cap, the recipe pinned in `recipeChoice` first and the good's other producers when the pin would loop (the breakdown's recipe book keeps them beside the pin) — and records how on every node (`BomLine.made`: recipe, machine, runs, circuit, held parts, cleanroom) with a machine chosen in the order owned → built by this line → one still needed; a good nothing can make there is `unmade`, by cause. A pooled pass over those choices sums every made good's demand over the whole list before rounding whole runs, which gives the build totals and one `make` build step per part, ordered inputs-first and batched by machine (`steps.ts`); only the tree shown stops at 12 levels. A placed line is broken down once more as a whole — the plan's own lines and the layout's parts together (Plan 5's `finishPlan`) — so its totals and part steps pool both. Stop items are priced at what making them on their own costs (add-on 12's machine prices too), and the recycling rule also catches smelting a finished part back into another material's ingot. Client: the Materials page shows "made in <machine> from <parts>" on every made row and offers the flow view's Swap recipe, extracted into a shared component.

**Tech Stack:** Node 24 (type stripping) · TypeScript 6.0.3 · highs 1.15.3 (through `lp.ts` only) · React 19.3.0 · vitest 5.0.3 (no new dependency).

## Architecture change requests

All of these become **one** architecture §11 entry, **41** (Task 1 Step 4):

1. `src/shared/types/plan.ts`: `interface BomMachine { mteId: number; name: string; tier: number; source: 'owned' | 'line' | 'new' | 'first' }` and `interface BomMade { recipeId: string; mapId: string; machine: BomMachine | 'crafting table'; runs: number; circuit?: number; nonConsumed?: StuffKey[]; cleanroom?: true }`; `BomLine.made?: BomMade` on every node of a good the breakdown makes (a node the tree cuts at its 12-level cap keeps it; never on a leaf); `runs` = that node's whole runs; `cleanroom` = the recipe needs one; `source`: `owned` = the group has one (world scan, `ownedManual`, a step's owned machines; reuse settings do not apply to a one-off job), `line` = a type this line adds, `new` = neither (the cheapest tier that runs it), `first` = only this line's own unit could, but its build needs this part. A good is made one way across the whole build list: a depth-first walk of its own, with no depth cap, chooses each good's recipe at its first visit (the pinned one first, else the cheapest; never one that consumes a good it is being made for) and memoizes it, so the choices form a DAG that the tree unfolds and the pooled pass sums. The breakdown's recipe book keeps a pinned good's normally kept producers beside the pinned recipe (`CandidateOptions.pinnedBeside`), so a pin that would loop there falls back to them; the line's own graph keeps today's rule (only the pin).
2. `BomLine.unmade?: 'no-recipe' | 'own-chain'` on every node and the total of a good the line cannot buy and the breakdown cannot make: `no-recipe` = nothing accepted at the tier makes it (import it); `own-chain` = every recipe the book holds for it consumes a good it is being made for (swap a recipe above it, or import it).
3. `Plan.buildAlternatives?: Record<StuffKey, PlanStep['alternatives']>`: the swap list of every made build part (every producer the breakdown's book holds — a pinned good's other producers too — by gross input cost per unit, then id, at most `MAX_ALTERNATIVES`, with `recipeParts`), so the Materials page offers the flow view's Swap recipe; pins stay `PlanSettings.recipeChoice[key]`, shared with the running line.
4. Pooled totals (amends §11.15 and plan 09's `BomLine.neededFor`): `Plan.buildTotals` and `expandBomLines(...).totals` sum each made good's demand over the choices' DAG for the whole list (below the tree's depth cap too) before rounding whole runs; `neededFor` amounts are each top-level line's exact (unrounded) share scaled to the pooled amount. The tree (`buildList` children) keeps whole runs per node.
5. `BuildStep.kind` gains `'make'`. After `gather`, one step per made build part: the top-level line's own `craft:<key>` id or `make:<key>`, kind `make` (a machine) or `craft` (a crafting table), and kind `gather` with id `make:<key>` or `craft:<key>` for an `unmade` part ("Get …", worded by its cause); ordered inputs first, a machine this line builds after its own craft and structure steps, batched by machine. Plan 5's `mergeBuildSteps` keeps `make` before the layout's steps (`BEFORE_LAYOUT`).
6. "We already make this" prices (amends spec §3.3 and plan 09's R7 for stop items): a stop item costs its key override, else what making it on its own costs (`standaloneCosts`: byproducts sunk) — in the line, over the cost model's graph and never through the target; in add-on 12's machine prices (`itemPrices`, which have no target), walked from the item alone — floored at its plain price when it is a GT material form or a molten fluid; its plain price when nothing at the tier makes it.
7. Recycling rule (`EXCLUSION_RULES.recycling` → `smeltsBack`) also catches a smelting recipe that melts a finished GT part (any prefix but the ore-processing forms and `ingot`/`nugget`/`block`) into GT forms of any material (130 recipes on the export: magnetic and pig-iron parts into their base metal), and one that smelts a non-GT item the pack makes from the output's material (214 of 428 non-GT smelts: bars, tools, machines; native clusters, poor ores, Red Iron Compound, wood and clay stay allowed).
8. Structure cells whose block id is in `NOTHING_TO_PLACE` need nothing placed: add-on 12's `structureParts` leaves them out (so machine build costs do too). Every build-list key is named by `partName` (`bom.ts`): the item's name when it has one other than its key; else, for a key that is some machine's `itemKey` (the lowest mteId when several), the machine's name; else `<name of <id>@0> (variant <meta>)`; else `<id>` for meta 0, else `<id> (meta <meta>)`.
9. A placed line's build (amends §11.15 and §11.16): `expandBomLines(pack, lines, settings, ctx, opts?: { steps?: readonly PlanStep[] })` — with `opts.steps` the machine pass knows the line's machines and the owned ones as `buildBom` does — returns, as optional additions, `makes?: BomMake[]` (the pooled make plan, Task 4) and `alternatives?: Record<StuffKey, PlanStep['alternatives']>`. Plan 5's `finishPlan` breaks the whole build down in one call — the plan's top-level lines without their children, less the casings the layout's hatches replace, then the layout's required parts, with the plan's steps — and `mergeLayoutIntoPlan` takes `buildList` (plus the optional lines), `buildTotals`, `buildAlternatives` and the `gather`, `craft` and `make` steps from it (`expanded.parts`, made by `steps.ts` `partSteps`), naming new keys with `partName`; an `expanded` without `parts` merges as today. The held parts and tools a make job names in its step (a mold, an extruder shape, a lens, a programmed circuit, a crafting tool) are not build-list lines; a make job that needs a cleanroom says so in its step and adds no cleanroom line.

**Rules.**
1. *One recipe per good: the choice walk.* Before any tree is built, a depth-first walk from the lines in build-list order chooses each good's recipe at its first visit and memoizes it; every node of the good uses it. The walk has **no depth cap**: each good is visited once, and the lineage (the goods on the walk's stack, which the good is being made for) bars every recipe that would consume one of them. At the visit of good G with lineage L: the candidates are `book.producersOf(G)` (the pinned recipe first); the usable ones consume no good of L ∪ {G}; the choice is the first usable one none of whose inputs loops back — an input loops back when it is no leaf, has no choice yet and every producer the book holds for it consumes a good of L ∪ {G} — else the first usable one; with none usable G gets no recipe (rule 4). The walk then visits the chosen recipe's inputs that have no choice yet, with G on the lineage. So a pin is used wherever it can be, and where it would loop the good falls back to its other producers as if nothing were pinned (the book holds them because of `pinnedBeside`): a pin never turns a part into an import. The choices form a DAG: a recipe never consumes a good on its stack, and a good chosen earlier elsewhere had its whole chain chosen then, with no good of the current stack in it. The tree (`lines` and their children) unfolds that DAG from each line with whole runs per node and stops at `MAX_DEPTH` (12): a node there keeps its `made` and has no children. The machine pass (rule 3) runs on the choices before the tree is built; Task 4 pools over the DAG, never over the cut tree.
2. *`made`* on every node of a good with a recipe (it has children, unless the recipe consumes nothing or the node is cut at `MAX_DEPTH`): `recipeId`; `mapId` = the producer's `LpRecipe.mapId`; `runs` = that node's whole runs; `circuit` = `recipeCircuit(recipe)` when defined; `nonConsumed` = the option `heldOption` keeps for each not-consumed slot (no avoid), the programmed circuit left out when `circuit` is set, omitted when empty; `cleanroom: true` when the recipe needs a cleanroom (`LpRecipe.needsCleanroom`); `machine` from rule 3.
3. *Machine* — a pass over the chosen goods once the walk is done, so a machine of this line can be refused when its own build needs the part: `owned = ownedForParts(ctx, settings, steps)` (the summed `ctx.ownedMachines`, `settings.ownedManual` and every step's `owned`; `declineReuse` and `reuseOwned` do not apply to a one-off job); `exclude` = the line types M for which the good is reached, through the choices, from a top-level line whose key is M's `itemKey` or a key of `structureParts(pack, M)`. `partMachine`: map `crafting` → `'crafting table'`; `furnace` → `{ mteId: PSEUDO_MACHINES.FURNACE, name: 'Furnace', tier: 0, source: 'owned' }`; else the machines of `mapId` that can run `r` at `groupTier` (`pickMachine`'s rule: a basic machine with `tier ≤ groupTier` and `V[tier] × map amps ≥ r.euPerTick`; any multiblock of the map), then the first of: one with `owned > 0` (highest tier, then lowest mteId) → `owned`; one in `line` and not in `exclude` (highest tier, lowest mteId) → `line`; a basic one not in `exclude` (lowest tier, lowest mteId — the cheapest to build) → `new`; a multiblock not in `exclude` (one in `multiblocks` first, lowest mteId) → `new`; an excluded one (lowest tier, lowest mteId) → `first`; none → `null`.

  ```ts
  /** One run of a good's chosen recipe: how much of the good it makes and what it consumes. */
  export interface PoolGood { perRun: number; inputs: ReadonlyArray<readonly [StuffKey, number]> }
  export interface Pooled {
    runs: Map<StuffKey, number>;                    // whole runs of every made good
    demand: Map<StuffKey, number>;                  // what the whole list needs of every good
    leaves: Map<StuffKey, number>;                  // goods that are not made: the pooled totals
    shares: Map<StuffKey, Map<StuffKey, number>>;   // leaf → top-level key → exact share (fractional runs)
    backEdges: Array<[StuffKey, StuffKey]>;         // [consumer, input] where the input was on the stack; sorted (its demand there is gathered)
  }
  export function poolMakes(tops: ReadonlyArray<{ key: StuffKey; amount: number }>, made: (key: StuffKey) => PoolGood | null): Pooled;
  /** One machine or crafting job of the build: the pooled runs of one made good. */
  export interface BomMake {
    key: StuffKey; made: BomMade /* runs = the pooled whole runs */; amount: number /* runs × made per run */;
    inputs: Array<{ key: StuffKey; amount: number }> /* consumed by all the runs, by key */;
    after: StuffKey[] /* made or unmade inputs but the input of a back edge from this good, and for a `line` machine its own key and structure parts that are top-level lines; sorted */;
  }
  ```
  and `Bom.makes: BomMake[]` (by key); `expandBomLines(...)` returns them as `makes?: BomMake[]` (optional addition to §11.15's shape). `Bom.totals` and `expandBomLines(...).totals` become `Pooled.leaves` (with have-counts and `unmade` as before).

**`poolMakes`** — the algorithm the signature leaves open:
1. Depth-first from the tops in order through `made(key)?.inputs`; post-order list; an input already on the stack is a back edge (`backEdges`; the breakdown's choices form a DAG, Task 3 rule 1, so it never passes one — only a `made` of the caller's own can).
2. Demand: every top's amount on its key; then the post-order reversed (consumers first): a made good with demand `d > TINY` runs `ceil(d / perRun − TINY)` times and adds `per × runs` to each input's demand (a back edge's to `leaves`); a good for which `made` returns null adds its `d` to `leaves`.
3. Shares: the same walk once per top, from that top alone, with fractional runs (`d / perRun`), adding each leaf amount to `shares[leaf][top.key]`.
4. Values `round9`.

In `bom.ts`: `poolMakes` runs over the top-level lines with `made(key)` = the good's choice (`{ perRun, inputs: book.inputsOf(lp) }`, null for a leaf or an unmade good) — the whole DAG, the goods below the tree's depth cap included; each total's `neededFor` amounts are its shares scaled by `pooled / Σ shares`, `round9`, by key, the last one taking the remainder, so they sum to the pooled amount as plan 09 requires; `Bom.makes` from `Pooled.runs` with the good's `made` (runs replaced by the pooled runs), and `after` without the input of a back edge from the good (its demand is gathered, so a step never waits for it). The tree (`lines` and children) keeps whole runs per node.
