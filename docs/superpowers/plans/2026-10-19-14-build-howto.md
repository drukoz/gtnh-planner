**Goal:** The user's report — *"what about the recipes for the electronic circuit where we are missing the molten tin recipe"* — fixed for the whole build list: every machine-made part of the "to build" list says what makes it, the build steps tell the group to make each one in order ("Make 4,032 L Molten Tin from 28× Tin Dust in a Fluid Extractor"), "we already make this" items stop being a cheap source the running line recycles into steel, structure cells that hold air or a fluid stop showing up as things to craft, and the build totals stop multiplying whole runs per part.

**Architecture:** Server: the build-list breakdown (`bom.ts` `expandWith`) makes each good one way for the whole list and records how (`BomLine.made`: recipe, machine, runs, circuit, held parts) with a machine chosen in the order owned → built by this line → one still needed; a pooled pass then sums every made good's demand over the whole list before rounding whole runs, which gives the build totals and one `make` build step per part, ordered inputs-first and batched by machine (`steps.ts`). Stop items are priced at what making them on their own costs, and the recycling rule also catches smelting a finished part back into another material's ingot. Client: the Materials page shows "made in <machine> from <parts>" on every made row and offers the flow view's Swap recipe, extracted into a shared component.

**Tech Stack:** Node 24 (type stripping) · TypeScript 6.0.3 · highs 1.15.3 (through `lp.ts` only) · React 19.3.0 · vitest 5.0.3 (no new dependency).

## Architecture change requests

All of these become **one** architecture §11 entry, **41** (Task 1 Step 4):

1. `src/shared/types/plan.ts`: `interface BomMachine { mteId: number; name: string; tier: number; source: 'owned' | 'line' | 'new' | 'first' }` and `interface BomMade { recipeId: string; mapId: string; machine: BomMachine | 'crafting table'; runs: number; circuit?: number; nonConsumed?: StuffKey[] }`; `BomLine.made?: BomMade` on every node the breakdown makes (never on a leaf), chosen by the ranking that chose its children; `runs` = that node's whole runs; `source`: `owned` = the group has one (world scan, `ownedManual`, a step's owned machines; reuse settings do not apply to a one-off job), `line` = a type this line adds, `new` = neither (the cheapest tier that runs it), `first` = only this line's own unit could, but its build needs this part. A good is made one way across the whole build list (the first visit's choice, memoized).
2. `BomLine.unmade?: true` on a node or total that the line cannot buy and nothing at the tier makes: it has to be imported.
3. `Plan.buildAlternatives?: Record<StuffKey, PlanStep['alternatives']>`: the swap list of every made build part (the breakdown's producers by gross input cost per unit, then id, at most `MAX_ALTERNATIVES`, with `recipeParts`), so the Materials page offers the flow view's Swap recipe; pins stay `PlanSettings.recipeChoice[key]`, shared with the running line.
4. Pooled totals (amends §11.15 and plan 09's `BomLine.neededFor`): `Plan.buildTotals` and `expandBomLines(...).totals` sum each made good's demand over the whole list before rounding whole runs; `neededFor` amounts are each top-level line's exact (unrounded) share scaled to the pooled amount. The tree (`buildList` children) keeps whole runs per node.
5. `BuildStep.kind` gains `'make'`. After `gather`, one step per made build part: the top-level line's own `craft:<key>` id or `make:<key>`, kind `make` (a machine) or `craft` (a crafting table), and kind `gather` with id `make:<key>` or `craft:<key>` for a part nothing at the tier makes ("Get …"); ordered inputs first, a machine this line builds after its own craft and structure steps, batched by machine. Plan 5's `mergeBuildSteps` keeps `make` before the layout's steps (`BEFORE_LAYOUT`).
6. "We already make this" prices (amends spec §3.3 and plan 09's R7 for stop items): a stop item costs its key override, else what making it on its own costs in the cost model's graph (`standaloneCosts`: byproducts sunk, never through the target), floored at its plain price when it is a GT material form or a molten fluid; its plain price when nothing at the tier makes it.
7. Recycling rule (`EXCLUSION_RULES.recycling` → `smeltsBack`) also catches a smelting recipe that melts a finished GT part (any prefix but the ore-processing forms and `ingot`/`nugget`/`block`) into GT forms of any material (130 recipes on the export: magnetic and pig-iron parts into their base metal), and one that smelts a non-GT item the pack makes from the output's material (214 of 428 non-GT smelts: bars, tools, machines; native clusters, poor ores, Red Iron Compound, wood and clay stay allowed).
8. Structure cells whose block id is in `NOTHING_TO_PLACE` need nothing placed: add-on 12's `structureParts` leaves them out (so machine build costs do too). A build-list key without an item name is named by `partName` (`bom.ts`): the item's name, else `<name of <id>@0> (variant <meta>)`, else `<id>` for meta 0, else `<id> (meta <meta>)`.

**Rules.**
1. *One recipe per good.* The breakdown chooses a good's producer at its first visit (depth-first, in build-list order, with today's lineage and loop-back rules at that visit) and memoizes it; every later node of the good uses it. A recipe pinned in `recipeChoice` is tried first; when it is not usable at that visit (it consumes a good of the lineage, or its inputs only loop back), the visit falls back to the ranked producers (`allProducersOf`) as if nothing were pinned, so a pin never turns a part into an import. A memoized recipe that consumes a good of a node's own lineage (possible only past `MAX_DEPTH`) makes that node a leaf. (The choices form no cycle: whichever of two goods is chosen first walks into the other with itself on the lineage.)
2. *`made`* on every node a recipe makes (it has children, unless the recipe consumes nothing): `recipeId`; `mapId` = the producer's `LpRecipe.mapId`; `runs` = that node's whole runs; `circuit` = `recipeCircuit(recipe)` when defined; `nonConsumed` = the option `heldOption` keeps for each not-consumed slot (no avoid), the programmed circuit left out when `circuit` is set, omitted when empty; `machine` from rule 3.
3. *Machine* — a second pass once every tree exists, so a machine of this line can be refused when its own build needs the part: `owned = ownedForParts(ctx, settings, steps)` (the summed `ctx.ownedMachines`, `settings.ownedManual` and every step's `owned`; `declineReuse` and `reuseOwned` do not apply to a one-off job); `exclude` = the line types M for which the good is a key in the subtree of a top-level line whose key is M's `itemKey` or a key of `structureParts(pack, M)`. `partMachine`: map `crafting` → `'crafting table'`; `furnace` → `{ mteId: PSEUDO_MACHINES.FURNACE, name: 'Furnace', tier: 0, source: 'owned' }`; else the machines of `mapId` that can run `r` at `groupTier` (`pickMachine`'s rule: a basic machine with `tier ≤ groupTier` and `V[tier] × map amps ≥ r.euPerTick`; any multiblock of the map), then the first of: one with `owned > 0` (highest tier, then lowest mteId) → `owned`; one in `line` and not in `exclude` (highest tier, lowest mteId) → `line`; a basic one not in `exclude` (lowest tier, lowest mteId — the cheapest to build) → `new`; a multiblock not in `exclude` (one in `multiblocks` first, lowest mteId) → `new`; an excluded one (lowest tier, lowest mteId) → `first`; none → `null`.
4. *Unmade:* a node that is no leaf of the oracle and has no usable producer, without having been cut by `MAX_DEPTH` (or whose recipe's machine is `null`), gets `unmade: true`, and so does its total.

  ```ts
  export interface PoolGood { perRun: number; inputs: ReadonlyArray<readonly [StuffKey, number]> }
  export interface Pooled {
    runs: Map<StuffKey, number>;                    // whole runs of every made good
    demand: Map<StuffKey, number>;                  // what the whole list needs of every good
    leaves: Map<StuffKey, number>;                  // goods that are not made: the pooled totals
    shares: Map<StuffKey, Map<StuffKey, number>>;   // leaf → top-level key → exact share (fractional runs)
    cycles: StuffKey[];                             // goods a back edge reached (their demand there is gathered)
  }
  export function poolMakes(tops: ReadonlyArray<{ key: StuffKey; amount: number }>, made: (key: StuffKey) => PoolGood | null): Pooled;
  /** One machine or crafting job of the build: the pooled runs of one made good. */
  export interface BomMake {
    key: StuffKey; made: BomMade /* runs = the pooled whole runs */; amount: number /* runs × made per run */;
    inputs: Array<{ key: StuffKey; amount: number }> /* consumed by all the runs, by key */;
    after: StuffKey[] /* made or unmade inputs, and for a `line` machine its own key and structure parts that are top-level lines; sorted */;

  ```

**`poolMakes`** — the algorithm the signature leaves open:
1. Depth-first from the tops in order through `made(key)?.inputs`; post-order list; an input already on the stack is a back edge (`cycles`; none occur unless the depth cap cut a lineage, Task 3 rule 1).
2. Demand: every top's amount on its key; then the post-order reversed (consumers first): a made good with demand `d > TINY` runs `ceil(d / perRun − TINY)` times and adds `per × runs` to each input's demand (a back edge's to `leaves`); a good for which `made` returns null adds its `d` to `leaves`.
3. Shares: the same walk once per top, from that top alone, with fractional runs (`d / perRun`), adding each leaf amount to `shares[leaf][top.key]`.
4. Values `round9`.

In `bom.ts`: `poolMakes` runs over the top-level lines with `made(key)` = the memoized producer (`{ perRun, inputs: book.inputsOf(lp) }`, null for a leaf or an unmade good); each total's `neededFor` amounts are its shares scaled by `pooled / Σ shares`, `round9`, by key, the last one taking the remainder, so they sum to the pooled amount as plan 09 requires; `Bom.makes` from `Pooled.runs` with the node's `made` (runs replaced by the pooled runs). - [ ] **Step 2: Write the audit**

`describe.skipIf(process.env.GTNH_LOCAL !== '1')('the build list of the user's Electronic Circuit line', …)`; `beforeAll`: `pack = await loadPack(activeExportDir(loadConfig()))`; `settings = normalizeSettings(JSON.parse(readFileSync('docs/local/ec-state.json', 'utf8')).settings)` (fail with "copy the user's settings to docs/local/ec-state.json (Task 7 Step 1)" when missing); `plan = await planLine(pack, settings, emptyContext())`, timed; `pricing = (await linePricing(pack, settings, emptyContext()))!`. Real keys: Steel Rod `gregtech:gt.metaitem.01@23305`, Steel Ingot `gregtech:gt.metaitem.01@11305`, Long Magnetic Steel Rod `gregtech:gt.metaitem.02@22355`, Steel Bars `dreamcraft:SteelBars@0`, Cupronickel Coil Block `gregtech:gt.blockcasings5@0`, Tin Dust `gregtech:gt.metaitem.01@2057`, Tin Ingot `gregtech:gt.metaitem.01@11057`, Empty Cell `IC2:itemCellEmpty@0`, Muffler Hatch (UHV) `gregtech:gt.blockmachines@99`.

- `it('plans the line in time')`: `status 'ok'`, under 5 s (`PLAN_SECONDS`).
- `it('says how the molten tin for the coils is made, before the coils')` (F1, the user's report): the build list holds the Cupronickel Coil Block (if not, the line changed: stop and report); every `fluid:molten.tin` node under it has `made` with a machine whose name contains `Fluid Extractor` and a Tin Dust or Tin Ingot child; the step `make:fluid:molten.tin` has kind `make`, matches `/^Make [\d,]+ L Molten Tin from [\d,]+× Tin (Dust|Ingot) in .*Fluid Extractor/` and comes before the coils' step (`craft:gregtech:gt.blockcasings5@0`).
- `it('gives every made part one step, after the parts it is made from')`: map step ids to keys (`make:<key>`, `craft:<key>`, `craft:<group>:<key>` for the four groups); every key with a `made` node has exactly one part step, of kind `make` for a machine and `craft` for a crafting table; every key with an `unmade` node has one of kind `gather`; for every part step, the steps of the made and unmade children of its key come earlier.
- `it('prices stopped items honestly and never smelts them back')` (F2): every stop item that is a raw input costs per unit (`cost / perSecond`) at least `pricing.model.costs.cost(key)` (weak duality: a dual price never exceeds a chain's) and, for a GT material form, at least `makePrices(pack, settings.costOverrides).price(key)`; the Steel Rod at least 0.5 (main 0.479, prototype 0.979). The export's smelting recipes Long Magnetic Steel Rod → Steel Ingot and Steel Bars → Steel Nugget exist and `EXCLUSION_RULES.recycling` holds for both. Following the plan's flows down from the Steel Rod import (raw → the steps that take it → their outputs → …), no step makes Steel Ingot; and no plan step's recipe is recycling.
- `it('lists no fluid cell and names every part')` (F3): no key in `buildList` (any depth) or `buildTotals` has its block id in `NOTHING_TO_PLACE`; every such key has a `plan.names` entry that is not the key; no step text contains `minecraft:`.
- `it('builds no hatch above what the structure needs')` (F4): every build-list key that is the item of a `kind: 'hatch'` machine has a tier at or below `plan.tier` or is `minimalHatch(pack, that machine)` itself; `gregtech:gt.blockmachines@99` appears nowhere (main listed it).
- `it('pools the build totals and keeps them in bounds')` (F4): for every total, the pooled amount is at most the sum of its leaf amounts in the tree (whole runs per node) + 1e-6; no total is a filled container (`pack.fluidContainers`) of `fluid:water`, `fluid:lava`, `fluid:air`, `fluid:steam` or `fluid:ic2steam` (main: 6,661 Water Cells, 443 Steam Cells); Empty Cell at most 64 (main 5,656; prototype 0 pooled, 17 per node); every free fluid (`FREE_KEYS`) at most 300,000 L (prototype: Air 230,000, Water 107,226); every other fluid at most 50,000 L (prototype: Light Oil 20,000; main: Benzene 1,420,000); every item at most 2,000 (prototype: Clay 1,156); the gather step names no `unmade` total. Print the gather text and the number of `make`, `craft` and `gather` part steps (`console.info`) and copy them, with the five largest totals, into the file's header comment.
