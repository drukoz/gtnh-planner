# World Power Fixes Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make the world scan's power estimate honest on the group's real base: steam that passes through tanks reaches the turbines, and battery buffers count their capacity.

**Architecture:** Two independent fixes found by Plan 4's real-world check (recorded in `docs/local/world-runs.md` of the main checkout): (1) Plan 4's steam graph in `src/server/world/power.ts` follows steam only through GT fluid pipes and boiler fronts, so a Railcraft tank between 77 high-pressure solar boilers and 15 steam turbines splits them and generation reads 0 EU/t; (2) the exporter writes `batterySlots: 0` and `amps: 0` for battery buffers (e.g. mID 182, 191, 193), so their `64 × V × slots` internal storage and amps are missing and stored EU exceeds capacity.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript world reader (`src/server/world/*`, `src/shared/world/*`), Java exporter (`exporter-mod/`, GTNH template, `GRADLE_USER_HOME=.local/gradle-home`), pack loader (`src/server/pack/*`), vitest with synthetic worlds (`tests/helpers/make-world.ts`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-17-gtnh-planner-design.md` §3.4–§3.5 (honest power estimate: clear/rain/average, stored EU, spare power); research `docs/research/gt-mechanics.md` §4 (turbines, solar boilers, battery buffers), `docs/research/world-format.md` §6 (tile entities), `docs/research/export-mod.md` §3.5 (prototype getters that throw or return defaults).

## Global Constraints

- Steam graph: fluid-holding blocks that pass steam between adjacent fluid pipes or machines count as nodes of the steam component — at least Railcraft tank parts (`Railcraft:machine.beta` valves/gauges/walls of one tank multiblock are one node: all parts of a tank connected face-to-face form one tank), GT super/quantum tanks, and other tank tile entities whose NBT holds fluid (`steam` or `ic2steam`). A tank joins every pipe end or machine face that touches any of its blocks. Multiblock steam producers (e.g. the Large Steel Boiler, export kind `multiblock` with a steam output hatch) feed the component through their output hatches.
- When a power network has steam turbines that get no steam the scan can follow, the scan adds the warning `"<N> steam turbines get no steam the scan can follow"` (one per network) instead of silently reporting 0.
- Battery buffers: the exporter writes the true slot count and amps for every `MTEBasicBatteryBuffer` prototype (read the constructor-set field or inventory size by reflection; never call methods that need a world); the loader fills `batterySlots` from the metaName/enum name (e.g. `…batterybuffer… 4x/8x/16x` or `BATTERY_BUFFER_<n>_BY_<n>_<TIER>`) when the export still says 0, and logs a pack warning for each fallback. Capacity = Σ battery max charge (from `Item.electric`) + `64 × V[tier] × slots`.
- Real data (GTNH_LOCAL=1): on a scan of the newest complete backup, the base's power networks report generation > 0 for clear weather and `storedEU ≤ capacityEU` on every network. Use a worktree-local config in `.local` (dataDir = this worktree's data, serverFolder and activeExportId copied from the main checkout's `data/config.json`), never the real config; a re-export for the exporter fix runs only through `npm run export` on the APFS clone exactly as Plan 1 defines (never the live server), and its new export id is recorded but the main checkout's active export is switched only by the controller.
- Commits end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`. No new dependencies.

## Review Focus

1. Boilers → pipe → tank → pipe → turbines yields non-zero steam and EU/t (synthetic test with exact numbers).
2. A tank shared by two separate pipe systems merges them into one steam component (and does not merge unrelated power networks of cables).
3. A turbine network with no reachable steam produces the warning, not a silent 0.
4. Battery buffer capacity is never smaller than its stored energy on the real world.
5. Old exports without the fix still load (fallback from names) with a warning.
